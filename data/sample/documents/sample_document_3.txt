OCEAN THERMAL SENSOR DEPLOYMENT REPORT
Station Gamma-7 | North Atlantic Monitoring Array

Abstract:
This document summarises oceanographic temperature readings collected by
autonomous underwater sensor buoys deployed at Station Gamma-7 in the
North Atlantic monitoring array. Sensor units recorded surface and
mid-depth thermal profiles at 15-minute intervals across a 90-day
observation window.

Key Findings:
- Mean sea-surface temperature: 14.3 degrees C (range: 11.1 to 17.8 degrees C)
- Thermocline depth: 85-110 m below surface
- Cold-water upwelling anomaly detected on days 42-51
- Salinity variance remained within normal operating bounds
- No calibration drift detected in instruments across the full period

Methodology:
CTD (Conductivity, Temperature, Depth) sensors were deployed via surface
tether at fixed coordinates 47.2 N, 31.5 W. Data was transmitted via
satellite link every 4 hours and stored in the monitoring system database.

Conclusions:
Station Gamma-7 data is consistent with expected seasonal thermal trends.
The upwelling anomaly requires further analysis in the subsequent cycle.
