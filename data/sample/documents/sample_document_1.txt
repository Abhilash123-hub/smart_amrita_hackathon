OCEAN THERMAL SENSOR DEPLOYMENT REPORT
Station Gamma-7 | North Atlantic Monitoring Array

Abstract:
This report summarises oceanographic temperature readings collected by
autonomous underwater sensor buoys deployed at Station Gamma-7 in the
North Atlantic monitoring array. Sensor units recorded surface and
mid-depth thermal profiles at fifteen-minute intervals across a 90-day
observation window.

Key Findings:
- Average sea-surface temperature: 14.3 degrees C (range: 11.1 to 17.8 degrees C)
- Thermocline depth: 85-110 m below surface
- Anomalous cold-water upwelling detected on days 42-51
- Salinity variance remained within normal operating bounds
- No instrument calibration drift detected across the full period

Methodology:
CTD (Conductivity, Temperature, Depth) probes were deployed via surface
tether at fixed coordinates 47.2 N, 31.5 W. Data was transmitted via
satellite uplink every four hours and stored in the monitoring database.

Conclusions:
Station Gamma-7 data is consistent with expected seasonal thermal
patterns. The upwelling event warrants further investigation in the
subsequent monitoring cycle.
