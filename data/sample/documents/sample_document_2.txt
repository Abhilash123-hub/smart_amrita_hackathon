AGRICULTURAL SOIL MOISTURE SENSOR DEPLOYMENT REPORT
Field Station Delta-3 | Northern Plains Monitoring Array

Abstract:
This report summarises soil moisture readings collected by autonomous
ground-based sensor units deployed at Field Station Delta-3 in the
Northern Plains monitoring array. Sensor units recorded surface and
sub-surface moisture profiles at fifteen-minute intervals across a
60-day observation window.

Key Findings:
- Average topsoil moisture content: 28.4 percent (range: 14.2 to 41.6 percent)
- Dry-layer depth: 30-50 cm below surface
- Anomalous moisture retention detected in plots 7-9 following precipitation event
- Nutrient variance remained within expected agricultural bounds
- No instrument calibration drift detected across the monitoring period

Methodology:
TDR (Time Domain Reflectometry) probes were deployed at fixed grid
coordinates across the study area. Data was transmitted via cellular
uplink every four hours and stored in the agricultural monitoring database.

Conclusions:
Field Station Delta-3 data shows moderate correlation with seasonal
precipitation patterns. The retention event in plots 7-9 warrants
agronomic review in the subsequent growing cycle.
